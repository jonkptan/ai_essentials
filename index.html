ACPR Method: Generic prompting method for completing tasks

Framework to instruct an AI chatbot to complete a specific task with relevant details for customised output.

Prompt template

  • Act as [insert chatbot's role]
  • Create [specify the task]
  • Present in [state the tone and style]
  • Refer to this: [include context, information, or examples]
Act as a tour guide from Japan. Create a 3-day itinerary to Hokkaido. Present in table format. Refer to this: - We will travel as a family of four, my wife and two teenage sons. - We like local food and cultural experiences. - We want to take scenic photos.
Act as a compassionate manager with experience providing constructive email feedback to a team member who missed a project deadline. Create an email addressing the missed deadline. Present in a calm, direct tone, professional, warm, and supportive. Refer to this: - Assume positive intent. This is a coaching moment, not a reprimand. - Open with empathy, ask clarifying questions, then close with support and next steps.
Ask Me Method: Getting AI to prompt you before generation

Get an AI chatbot to ask interactive questions to clarify the task and gather relevant information. This helps the chatbot understand your needs and provide a customised response.

Prompt template

  • Act as [insert chatbot's role]
  • Create [specify the task]
  • Ask me [x] multiple-choice questions, one question at a time, to provide the best and most tailored response.
Act as an expert in report writing. Create a proposal for the adoption of AI in my company, [Company Name]. Ask me five multiple-choice questions, one question at a time, to provide the best and most tailored response.

App Ideas for Practice

1. Quotation Comparator & Negotiation Planner

Input: Upload supplier quotations in PDF, Word, or text format.

Output: Side-by-side comparison of prices, terms, and conditions, plus a suggested negotiation plan.

Benefit: Users see differences quickly and receive a ready negotiation strategy.

Prompt: You are an AI assistant that compares supplier quotations. When the user uploads quotations in PDF format, output a side-by-side comparison table of prices, terms, and conditions. Then generate a negotiation plan highlighting leverage points and recommended talking points.

2. Smart Email Assistant (Responder + Translator)

Input: Paste an incoming email, select a tone, and choose an optional translation language.

Output: Suggested replies in the chosen tone, with translation when selected.

Benefit: Staff respond quickly in the right tone and language.

Prompt: You are an AI assistant that generates email responses. When the user pastes an email, provide suggested replies in the selected tone, such as formal, friendly, or persuasive. Add dropdown options for tone, brevity, and language, plus a field for words or phrases to include. When a target language is selected, translate the reply naturally.

3. Contract Risk Analyzer

Input: Upload a contract or clause.

Output: Plain-English summary, risk rating, and recommended action.

Benefit: Staff gain clarity and risk awareness without legal expertise.

Prompt: You are an AI assistant that analyses contracts. When the user uploads a contract or clause, output a plain-English summary, assign a low, medium, or high risk rating, and suggest actions for contract negotiation.

4. Shipment Document Summarizer

Input: Upload shipping documents such as a bill of lading or packing list.

Output: Summary of origin, destination, vessel, ETA, and special notes.

Benefit: Users obtain key details without reading long documents.

Prompt: You are an AI assistant that summarises shipment documents. When the user uploads shipping files, extract and output the origin, destination, vessel, ETA, and any special notes.

5. Expense Report Generator

Input: Upload receipts or expense statements.

Output: Categorised expense report with totals for each category and the top three spending items.

Benefit: Managers receive an immediate financial overview.

Prompt: You are an AI assistant that generates expense reports. When the user uploads receipts or statements in PDF or image format, categorise expenses such as transport, storage, and administration. Output a summary table with totals for each category and highlight the top three spending items.

6. Quality Report Interpreter

Input: Upload a laboratory test or inspection report.

Output: Simplified summary with grade, issues, recommendations, and a pass or fail flag.

Benefit: Managers make quick decisions without reading technical details.

Prompt: You are an AI assistant that interprets quality reports. When the user uploads laboratory or inspection notes, output the grade, issues, recommendations, and a simple pass or fail flag.

7. Supplier Comparison Dashboard

Input: Upload multiple supplier profiles or quotations.

Output: Dashboard comparing prices, delivery times, and reliability scores.

Benefit: Staff see which supplier is most competitive.

Prompt: You are an AI assistant that compares suppliers. When the user uploads supplier documents, output a dashboard comparing prices, delivery times, and reliability. Rank the suppliers and highlight the best option with reasons.

8. Market News Digest

Input: Enter keywords or an industry.

Output: Condensed digest of key points and likely impact.

Benefit: Users save time and receive actionable insights.

Prompt: You are an AI assistant that summarises market news. When the user enters keywords or an industry, search for news from the past week. Output a digest of key points, explain the likely impact, and recommend actions.

9. Customer Communication Log Summarizer

Input: Upload customer emails or chat transcripts.

Output: Summary of concerns, requests, and sentiment trends.

Benefit: Managers see what customers request or complain about.

Prompt: You are an AI assistant that summarises customer communications. When the user uploads multiple emails or transcripts, output the main concerns, requests, and overall sentiment trends.

10. Invoice Parser

Input: Upload invoices in PDF or image format.

Output: Structured data extracted from the uploaded invoices.

Benefit: Users reduce manual data entry.

Prompt: You are an AI assistant that extracts data from invoices. When the user uploads multiple invoices in PDF or image format, output the invoice details in CSV format. Include a button for users to download the parsed data as a CSV file.

Clear Writing Instructions

Use

  • Use clear, simple language.
  • Use short, impactful sentences.
  • Use active voice.
  • Focus on practical, actionable insights.
  • Use bullet lists in social media posts.
  • Support claims with data and specific examples when possible.
  • Address the reader as “you” and “your.”
  • Vary sentence length and structure naturally.
  • Prefer specific nouns, numbers, examples, and actions.
  • Write like a person with a point of view.

Avoid

  • Em dashes and semicolons.
  • Passive voice, metaphors, clichés, hashtags, and broad generalisations.
  • Repeated contrast formulas and forced groups of three.
  • Generic setup or closing language.
  • Warnings, notes, filler, and unnecessary context.
  • Restating the request or repeating the same idea.
  • Excessive headings, transitions, qualifiers, and parenthetical explanations.
  • Rhetorical questions and fake conversational transitions.
  • Corporate language when ordinary language works.
  • Generic claims without a concrete example, reason, number, person, event, or action.
  • Social icons, hashtags, asterisks, and markdown in the requested output.

Avoid these words and phrases

can, may, just, that, very, really, literally, actually, certainly, probably, basically, could, maybe, delve, embark, enlightening, esteemed, shed light, craft, crafting, imagine, realm, game-changer, unlock, discover, skyrocket, abyss, not alone, in a world where, revolutionize, disruptive, utilize, utilizing, dive deep, tapestry, illuminate, unveil, pivotal, intricate, elucidate, hence, furthermore, however, harness, exciting, groundbreaking, cutting-edge, remarkable, it, remains to be seen, glimpse into, navigating, landscape, stark, testament, in summary, in conclusion, moreover, boost, skyrocketing, opened up, powerful, inquiries, ever-evolving.

Advanced Prompting Techniques

Open each technique to view its purpose and example.

Adv Technique 1. Hashtag Prompting

Use concise, keyword-driven hashtags, such as #professional, #summarize, and #bulletpoints, to quickly set the tone, format, or function of the output.

Example:

Create an email to a client explaining the project delay. #CommsExpertPOV #concise #professional #apologetic #ProjectAlpha #delay3weeks

Note: This method is a shortcut to writing ACPR prompts.

Adv Technique 2. Chain-of-Thoughts (CoT) Prompting

A CoT prompt guides the model through a defined step-by-step process, breaking a complex problem into smaller logical parts before providing the final answer. This supports accuracy and transparency in problem-solving.

Example:

Act as a compassionate manager with experience providing constructive feedback to a team member who missed a project deadline. Create an email addressing the missed deadline. Break this down step-by-step: 1. First, identify an empathetic opening that acknowledges the impact without blame. 2. Then, formulate two or three clarifying questions to understand the root barrier. 3. Finally, propose specific support or resources to prevent recurrence. Present in a calm, direct tone, professional, warm, and supportive. Refer to this: - Assume positive intent. This is a coaching moment, not a reprimand. - Open with empathy, ask clarifying questions, then close with support and next steps.
Adv Technique 3. Leveling-Up Prompting

Ask the AI to rate the quality of its previous output, or provide your own rating, then ask it to level up by improving a specific aspect such as complexity, creativity, or detail.

Examples:

Rate your previous output from 1 to 10 in terms of [specific aspect]. Improve it to 10. Or: This output was Level 1 for creativity. Rewrite it to achieve Level 2 by using more descriptive language and an example.
Adv Technique 4. Prompt Templates Creation

Capture the nuance of a refined, multi-step AI conversation in one reusable, high-quality prompt.

Example:

You helped me create [describe the final output, for example, a 250-word technical abstract on AI ethics]. Analyse this conversation and write one complete prompt that would reproduce this result directly so I can reuse it as a template for future tasks.
